A few useful answers

Here to help.

Questions or something not working? Email amiibodex@huls.es. Include your device model, app version and what happened. TestFlight testers can also use Send Beta Feedback.

Do I need to scan my amiibo?

No. You can browse and manually track your collection on iPhone or iPad. NFC scanning is optional and requires a compatible physical iPhone.

What does Dex-Verified mean?

It records an NFC tag read when you add the identified amiibo as owned, including the scan date. It does not authenticate the figure, packaging or ownership.

Where is my collection saved?

Your collection is saved on your device. Optional Game Center scores are associated with your player. Artwork fallbacks and shopping links may contact external services. Read the privacy policy.

When can I download the app?

Version 1.0 is in TestFlight testing for iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. We’ll add an App Store link here when it launches.
