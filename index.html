Do I need the physical amiibo to use the app?+

No. Browse the catalog and manually track owned and wishlisted entries on iPhone or iPad. NFC scanning is optional and requires a compatible physical iPhone.

What does "Dex-Verified" mean?+

Dex-Verified records an NFC tag read when you add the identified amiibo as owned. It includes a scan date. It does not authenticate the figure, packaging, seller or ownership.

Which devices are supported?+

iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. NFC scanning requires a compatible physical iPhone; iPad supports browsing and manual collection tracking.

Is my collection data private?+

Your collection is saved on your device. If you opt into Game Center, aggregate scores are associated with your Game Center player—not an anonymous identity. Artwork fallbacks can contact Nintendo or GitHub, and shopping links open external sites.

Are the shopping links affiliate links?+

Some "Buy" links may be affiliate links that help support development, at no extra cost to you. This is disclosed in the app next to the buttons and in Settings.
